# crime_data_analysis
from the raw to meaningful dashboard with decision making

summary points:
1. Total incidents analyzed: 5,050
2. Districts covered: 10 (North, South, Central, West, Midtown, East, Southwest, Northeast, Northwest, Southeast)
3. Crime types tracked: Fraud, Arson, Drug Offense, Theft/Larceny, Kidnapping, Domestic Violence, and others
4. Total property loss recorded: $110,435,918
5. Case status breakdown: Closed (1,099) · Open (1,096) · Under Investigation (1,073) · Pending (713) · Unknown (696) · Resolved (373)
6. Average severity score range: ~2.57–2.61 across districts, fairly evenly distributed (no single district is dramatically worse on severity alone)
7. Districts with most open cases: North (158), South (142), Midtown (113)
8. Data cleaning applied: missing/"Unknown" values in date, time, severity, case status, resolution, weapon, arrests, property loss, and
   reporting-channel fields were filled using date-of-cleaning and majority-value logic before analysis.

Key Business Insights

1. Highest-crime district: North — 696 incidents (~13.8% of all cases), the busiest district city-wide.
2. Most common crime type: Fraud — 376 cases, ahead of Arson (352) and Drug Offense (350).
3. Peak crime period: Midnight (12 AM) — 351 incidents, the single busiest hour of the day, well ahead of the next-highest hour (234 at 2 AM).
4. Highest property-loss crime: Fraud — $8.15M total — Fraud leads both in frequency and dollar loss, ahead of Kidnapping ($7.85M) and Drug Offense ($7.72M).
5. Case resolution rate: 29.2% — only 1,472 of 5,050 cases are Closed or Resolved; the majority sit in Open, Pending, Under Investigation, or Unknown status — a clear operational bottleneck worth flagging to leadership.
